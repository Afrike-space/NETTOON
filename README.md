🌟 Welcome to Nettoon

Nettoon is the world’s first free video streaming platform created exclusively for cartoons and animations.
Born from a passion for storytelling, imagination, and creativity, Nettoon exists to give animation lovers around the world a true home — one designed by dreamers, for dreamers.

These are the first official lines of code behind a new digital universe —
a space where childhood wonder meets modern technology,
and where the art of animation finally has its own stage.

#🚀 What is Nettoon?

Nettoon is a free, user-driven streaming application dedicated to cartoons and animated content —
a community-powered platform where you can:

📤 Upload your own animations and short films.

📺 View & Stream high-quality animated content, anytime and anywhere.

🔗 Share your favorite shows and clips with the world.

From nostalgic classics to new-age animations —
Nettoon is your one-stop animation universe.

#🎯 Our Vision

“To build the world’s most vibrant community for animated storytelling.”

For decades, animation lovers have had to search through general video platforms,
lost amidst unrelated content.

Nettoon changes that.
We are creating a dedicated digital world for animation lovers —
a focused, creative, and community-driven space that celebrates the art of imagination.

#⚖️ What Nettoon Is — and Isn’t

✅ Only Cartoons & Animations — nothing else.
This keeps our community clean, creative, and true to purpose.

🚫 No live-action, no vlogs, no noise.
Just pure animated brilliance.

By keeping the platform focused, we ensure a seamless experience that respects both creators and viewers.
